About the game

Journey from the classroom to the stars: travel by chopper, crack codes in the computer room, survive cannon battles, and face the final alien showdown.

Your overall time and rank — Student, Professor, or Saviour — depend on how fast and how well you play. Practice modes let you rehearse tough rooms without affecting your main run.

Built as a single-player desktop adventure with light educational goals. No microtransactions — just the full game when it launches.
